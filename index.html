The Analyst Files · Case #05
THE VANISHING CABS
Ride-Hailing

Setup

You're a Product Analyst on an Uber-style platform. Complaints flood the City–Airport corridor: riders can't find cabs, drivers who accept then cancel. The supply-demand gap bleeds goodwill and revenue. You've been handed a notebook with 6,745 ride requests and told to find the root cause.

Evidence

  • Cancellation and non-availability cluster on City → Airport and Airport → City routes.
  • The problem appears at specific times of day — but no one has quantified exactly when.
  • Complaints differ: drivers cite unprofitable return trips; riders cite long wait times.

Section 2

Real-World Use Cases

Exploratory AnalysisBefore Airbnb investigates a booking-rate drop, it runs an identical first pass — distribution by market, property type, booking window — to define the shape before the deep dive.
Feature EngineeringA logistics platform extracted “hours-since-last-delivery” and found driver fatigue past the 8-hour mark tripled cancellations. That feature didn't exist in raw data.
Direct vs. Root CauseA food app saw late-delivery spikes (direct cause). The root cause — found via feature engineering — was orders concentrated in a postal code with no dark kitchen nearby.
Data-driven RecommendationsUber's real “Airport mode” let drivers opt into airport queues with guaranteed return matching — a data-driven root cause turned into a product feature.
Competitor AnalysisWhen Swiggy saw delivery time rise in Tier-2 cities and Zomato didn't, that told them the cause was internal (partner allocation), not external (traffic).

Prerequisites to Refresh

Make sure these from RCA — I are fresh — today applies the same life cycle to a real dataset; the reasoning becomes code.

RCA Life Cycle (CRIED)
Clarify → Rule Out → Internal → External → Diagnose → Conclude. Every step maps to a section of today's notebook.
Pandas groupby & aggregation
Cancellation rate by route (groupby + count + divide) and by hour-of-day are the two most common operations today. Warm up with one groupby.
Filtering & segmentation
The in-depth analysis slices by route, time slot, and driver status at once. Multi-condition filtering is the core tool — be comfortable with it.
Direct vs. root cause
Today formalizes a concept from RCA — I: what you see is usually a direct cause; the root cause is one level deeper.
